## Rules

1. **Own-and-annotate** two books per quarter; library/summary the rest.
2. Every book entry gets a 10-line synthesis note (`notes/concepts/books/`) — otherwise it was entertainment.
3. Free-online canon (Molnar, Barocas, Hamilton, FPP3) is fully readable in-browser; prioritize over paywalled equivalents.
4. Read López de Prado critically: the backtesting chapters are canon; parts of the ML chapter drew substantive community critique — capture both sides in your note.
5. Books age slower than blogs but faster than regulations; re-check editions at annual review.
